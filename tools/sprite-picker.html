1 · pick a shape

Click a shape - it selects the one actually painted at that pixel, which is the one you can see. Its vertices appear as handles.

2 · edit its vertices

Click a handle to select it, shift-click for more. Drag to move one - it snaps to the grid the format stores. Delete or the button below removes the selected handles, which is how you straighten an edge that curves inward.
